平均来看，Tier 1 每篇贵将近 3 倍，但曝光多将近 4 倍，所以每千次曝光的成本反而比较低；Tier 2 适合铺量、覆盖更多不同的受众。On average a Tier 1 post costs nearly 3x more but brings nearly 4x the impressions, so its cost per 1,000 impressions is lower; Tier 2 is for volume and reaching more different audiences.
