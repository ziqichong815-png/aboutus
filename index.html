公司简介Who we are

MKTG Stories Solutions（行销日志）是一家马来西亚的数码营销公司，服务餐饮、生活消费和企业品牌。我们把品牌想说的话，做成内容、KOC 合作和广告，再用系统把每一档的结果记录下来。 MKTG Stories Solutions is a Malaysian digital marketing company serving food and beverage, lifestyle and corporate brands. We turn what a brand wants to say into content, KOC partnerships and ads, then record the result of every campaign in a system.

我们从小红书 KOC 探店活动做起，一步步把 TikTok、LEMON8、Instagram 和广告投放放进同一套打法。今天我们既是品牌的营销团队，也是品牌的系统搭建伙伴：一档 campaign 结束后，留下来的不只是一份报告，还有下次可以直接使用的名单、数据和流程。 We started with Xiaohongshu KOC store-visit campaigns and have since brought TikTok, LEMON8, Instagram and paid ads into one approach. We work as a brand's marketing team and as its systems partner: when a campaign ends, you keep more than a report. You keep the creator list, the data and the process for the next one.

  • 马来西亚在地，华语与双语市场Local to Malaysia, Chinese and bilingual audiences 合作博主来自本地 KOC 网络，内容和档期按马来西亚消费者的节庆与习惯安排。Our creators come from a local KOC network, and content follows the festivals and habits of Malaysian consumers.
  • 数据看得见Numbers you can see 每个客户有自己的专属看板和中英文报告，只看得到自己的数据。Each client gets a private dashboard and bilingual reports that show only their own data.
  • 自己搭系统，所以能快速定制We build our own tools, so we can tailor fast 我们用 Vibe Coding 自己开发工具，可以照品牌的流程做，不用硬套现成软件。We build tools with vibe coding, shaped around your workflow instead of forcing an off-the-shelf product.

我们帮品牌用内容、博主和系统做增长。从一档节庆 campaign，到一整套自己的营销系统，我们把想法变成成果。 We help brands grow with content, creators and systems. From one seasonal campaign to a full marketing system of your own, we turn ideas into results.
